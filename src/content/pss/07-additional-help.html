Additional Work Is Available Under a Separate Agreement

Some Situations Require More Than a Conversation.

The Private Strategy Session helps you understand your situation, discuss potential approaches, and determine appropriate next steps.

In some cases, moving forward may require additional work.

Examples include

  • Custom document preparation
  • Plan amendments or specialized agreements
  • Extensive transaction or document analysis
  • Formal written legal opinions
  • Corrective filings or compliance-related work
  • Transaction implementation
  • IRS correspondence or representation
  • Other specialized services

How Additional Services Work

  1. 1If Tim determines that your situation would benefit from additional services, we can discuss the proposed work, its scope, and the applicable fees.
  2. 2Any additional services will be quoted separately and provided under an appropriate written agreement.
  3. 3No additional work begins and no additional fees are charged without your authorization.

Your Private Strategy Session is a complete consultation service in its own right. Additional services are offered only when appropriate to your situation.
