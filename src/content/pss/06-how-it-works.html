The Process

How Your Private Strategy Session Works

Four steps from booking to a clear direction forward.

  1. 1
    Step 01

    Choose Your Time and Reserve Your Session

    Select an available appointment, verify your Tax Academy member rate if applicable, and complete your secure booking.

    If you have an eligible $125 consultation credit, apply it before paying.

  2. 2
    Step 02

    Tell Tim About Your Situation

    After booking, complete a brief intake form explaining your circumstances, the primary issue, and what you want to accomplish.

    You can securely submit selected documents or background materials that may help Tim prepare.

  3. 3
    Step 03

    Work Through It Directly With Tim

    Meet privately with Tim for up to 45 minutes to discuss your situation, relevant considerations, available approaches, and potential next steps.

  4. 4
    Step 04

    Leave With Direction

    Determine your next steps and receive a concise follow-up summary of the discussion.

    If additional research, document preparation, formal analysis, or implementation is needed, those services can be considered under a separate agreement.

The purpose is not to fill the clock.

If the central issue can be addressed in less than 45 minutes, that is a successful session. The price reflects dedicated access, preparation, and the nature of the service, not a guarantee that every appointment will last exactly 45 minutes.
