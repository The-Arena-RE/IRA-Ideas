4. ATTORNEY-CLIENT RELATIONSHIP AND PROSPECTIVE-CLIENT INFORMATION

Tim Berry is an attorney. However, visiting the website, reading website content, joining an invitation list, submitting an event application, scheduling an appointment, or sending information does not by itself create an attorney-client relationship.

An attorney-client relationship is created only when:

  • IRA Ideas agrees to the representation
  • Any required conflict review is completed
  • The scope of the representation is established
  • The parties enter into any required written engagement agreement

Information provided by a person seeking legal assistance may be subject to duties owed to prospective clients under applicable law. However, submitting information does not guarantee that IRA Ideas can represent you or that an attorney-client relationship will be formed.

Before IRA Ideas confirms an engagement, do not send:

  • Social Security numbers
  • Full financial- or retirement-account numbers
  • Passwords or security codes
  • Complete tax returns
  • Unredacted identification
  • Highly sensitive legal documents
  • Information you would not want disclosed if a conflict prevents representation

Information submitted before an engagement is confirmed may be used to:

  • Respond to you
  • Understand the general nature of the matter
  • Conduct a conflict review
  • Decide whether IRA Ideas can assist
  • Schedule a consultation
  • Prepare an event or educational session

For existing clients, the applicable engagement agreement and professional duties may provide protections beyond this Privacy Policy. When those protections are greater, the greater protection controls.
