01
Is 15 minutes really enough?
For many specific retirement-plan questions, a focused conversation can provide useful clarity and direction. Some situations require additional research or more extensive discussion. If that applies to your situation, Tim can explain your available next steps.
02
Can I ask about a transaction that already happened?
Yes. You can bring a specific question about a past transaction, potential mistake, or compliance concern. Comprehensive transaction analysis and detailed document review are separate services when required.
03
Is this only for Self-Directed IRAs?
No. This consultation is available for both Self-Directed IRAs and Self-Directed 401(k)s, including Solo 401(k) plans.
04
What if I haven't made an investment yet?
That's a good time to ask. You can clarify a particular rule, potential concern, or next step before completing a transaction.
05
Can I ask about Checkbook LLCs, real estate, or private lending?
Yes. All of these subjects can be discussed. The consultation will focus on your primary concern rather than an extensive review of every aspect of the investment or structure.
06
What if I already know my situation is complicated? Do I have to book the $125 session first?

No. You can book a Private Strategy Session directly without purchasing the $125 consultation first. If you already know your situation requires advanced planning or an extensive discussion, that is the right place to start.

The Private Strategy Session provides up to 45 minutes with Tim and allows you to submit relevant background information and documents.

07
What if I need more than 15 minutes?

If more extensive assistance is appropriate, you can upgrade to a Private Strategy Session.

Your entire $125 consultation payment can be applied toward that session when booked within 14 days of completing your initial consultation. You only pay the remaining difference based on your applicable member or standard rate.

08
Does the $125 session include document preparation or corrective work?

No. The Retirement Certainty Session provides focused consultation and guidance.

If your situation requires custom paperwork, formal document review, corrective work, implementation, or other specialized services, those services will be discussed and quoted separately under an appropriate written scope of engagement.

No additional services will be performed or charged without authorization.

09
Why is the consultation only $125?

Not every retirement-plan question requires an extensive engagement.

This focused consultation makes it easier to get experienced guidance on a specific concern without paying for a longer session you may not need.
