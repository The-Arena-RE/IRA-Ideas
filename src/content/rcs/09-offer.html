Introducing the Retirement Certainty Session

The Retirement Certainty Session is a focused, up to 15-minute consultation designed to help you better understand one specific retirement-plan question, transaction, or concern.

Not every question requires a lengthy engagement. Sometimes you just need the right experience applied to the right issue.

1
Bring Your Primary Question
Tell Tim what you're concerned about, what you've done, or what you're considering. Focus the discussion on your most important question.
2
Understand the Relevant Rules and Considerations
Discuss how relevant retirement-plan rules may apply to your situation and identify potential concerns that deserve attention.
3
Determine Your Next Step
Leave with a clearer understanding of the issue and what to consider next. If additional research, documentation, or more extensive analysis is needed, Tim can discuss the appropriate next step.
