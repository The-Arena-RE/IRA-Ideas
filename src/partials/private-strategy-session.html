    What can I bring to the session?

    Bring the transaction, structure, question, document, strategy, or problem you want to work through. You can also submit relevant documents before your session.

    Is 45 minutes enough?

    Most situations may not require the full 45 minutes. The session allows up to 45 minutes so there is enough time to understand your situation and work through the relevant questions without rushing.

    What if my question is answered in 15 minutes?

    Then we accomplished the objective. You're paying for dedicated access to Tim's experience and help working through the issue, not for Tim to fill a clock.

    Can I send documents before the session?

    Yes. Relevant documents can be securely submitted during the booking process.

    I'm a Tax Academy member. Can I still email Tim?

    Yes. Tax Academy members can still submit questions by email at no additional charge. The Private Strategy Session is for clients who want dedicated live time with Tim.

    Can Tim help with implementation after the call?

    If your situation requires additional work, services, or implementation, available next steps can be discussed separately.

    I'm not a Tax Academy member. Can I still book?

    Yes. Standard Private Strategy Sessions are available for $1,500.

If your membership is active, the page will show the $1,000 member rate and booking form. If no active membership is found, it will show the $1,500 standard rate and booking form. You do not need to leave IRA Ideas or log in to the Tax Academy.

    Can I use a coupon code for the member price?
